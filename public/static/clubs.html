Frequently Asked Questions

Students can sign up for clubs at the beginning of each semester during Club Fair, or by contacting the club advisor directly. Permission forms are available in the main office and must be signed by a parent or guardian.

Yes! Students are encouraged to explore multiple interests. However, we recommend limiting participation to 2-3 clubs to ensure they can commit fully to each activity while maintaining academic balance.

Most clubs are free to join. Some activities like sports teams or specialized clubs may have minimal fees for uniforms, equipment, or competition entry. Financial assistance is available for families who need support.

Please notify the club advisor in advance if your child will be absent. Regular attendance is important for some clubs, especially competitive teams, but we understand that conflicts occasionally arise.
