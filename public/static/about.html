Welcome to Avoca School District 37

Avoca School District 37 is a small, suburban public school district located in the village of Wilmette, Illinois. Our district serves approximately 750 students in grades Pre-K through 8 across two schools: Avoca West Elementary and Marie Murphy Middle School.

Since our founding, we have been committed to providing an exceptional educational experience that nurtures the whole child—academically, socially, and emotionally. Our dedicated faculty and staff work tirelessly to create an environment where every student can thrive and reach their full potential.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Avoca School District 37 is committed to developing engaged learners who demonstrate respect for themselves, others, and the world around them. We achieve this through challenging academics, meaningful relationships, and purposeful opportunities.

Our Vision

Every student will be empowered to pursue their passions, discover their strengths, and become confident, caring contributors to society. We envision schools where curiosity drives learning, diversity is celebrated, and excellence is achieved through collaboration and innovation.

Our History

1928

District Founded

Avoca School District 37 was established to serve the growing community of Wilmette.

1955

Avoca West Opens

The new elementary school opened its doors to serve grades K-5.

1972

Marie Murphy School

Marie Murphy Middle School was established to serve grades 6-8.

2010

Facility Renovations

Major renovations brought modern learning spaces and technology upgrades.

Today

Continuing Excellence

We continue our tradition of excellence, serving over 750 students annually.

Board of Education

The Avoca School District 37 Board of Education is a seven-member elected body that governs our district. Board members serve four-year terms and are responsible for setting district policy, approving budgets, and ensuring the district fulfills its mission.

Board Meetings

Regular board meetings are held on the third Tuesday of each month at 6:30 PM in the District Office Board Room. All meetings are open to the public.

Frequently Asked Questions

Avoca School District 37 serves students in Pre-Kindergarten through 8th grade. Avoca West Elementary serves Pre-K through 5th grade, while Marie Murphy Middle School serves grades 6-8.

To enroll your child, please contact the District Office at (847) 251-3587 or visit our enrollment page. You will need to provide proof of residency, your child's birth certificate, and immunization records.

School hours are 8:15 AM to 3:00 PM for both Avoca West and Marie Murphy. Before and after school care programs are available for families who need extended hours.

Yes, bus transportation is provided for students who live more than 1.5 miles from their school. Routes and schedules are published before the start of each school year.

There are many ways to get involved! Join the PTO, volunteer in classrooms, attend board meetings, or participate in school events. Contact your school's office to learn about current volunteer opportunities.

We offer a comprehensive range of programs including gifted education, special education services, ESL support, art, music, physical education, and STEM enrichment. After-school activities include sports, robotics, drama, and more.
