Common Questions

The district office is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM during the school year. Summer hours may vary - please call ahead during June, July, and August.

Please contact your child's school directly before 9:00 AM. Avoca West can be reached at (847) 251-3617 and Marie Murphy at (847) 251-3527. You can also report absences through the Parent Portal.

For transportation questions or concerns, please contact the District Office at (847) 251-3587 and ask for the transportation coordinator. For morning emergencies, call before 7:00 AM.

We'd love to show you our schools! Please contact the District Office to schedule a tour. We offer tours during school hours so prospective families can see our programs in action.
